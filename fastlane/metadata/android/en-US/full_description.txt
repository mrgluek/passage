Passage runs a local SOCKS5 proxy on 127.0.0.1. Messaging apps on the phone that support a SOCKS5 proxy connect to it, and Passage picks the best available connection for them automatically.

* Set up with a passage:// link: paste it, or tap it to open the app.
* Copy proxy link sets up another app in one paste.
* The proxy uses a random port, username and password made on the phone.
* Status screen, Check now, and a report you can send from the app.
